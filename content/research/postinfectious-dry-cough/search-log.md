## Search conclusions that changed the synthesis

1. A 2024 CMAJ review describes postinfectious cough as affecting about 11–25% of adults after respiratory infection, lasting 3–8 weeks, and finds no convincing evidence that routine inhaled corticosteroids, bronchodilators or oral medicines improve undifferentiated postinfectious cough.
2. Australian CICADA guidance supports a cause-directed approach and recommends chest X-ray plus spirometry once cough is chronic (>8 weeks in adults), with specialist referral for unexplained/refractory chronic cough.
3. National Asthma Council Australia recommends indoor relative humidity around 30–50% and says humidifiers are not usually recommended for everyday use unless the environment is very dry or a clinician advises them.
4. CHEST reflux-cough guidance recommends avoiding food within 3 hours of bedtime and recommends against PPI monotherapy for cough without heartburn/regurgitation.
5. Speech pathology/behavioural cough suppression has RCT and meta-analytic support in refractory/unexplained chronic cough, but this evidence is indirect for a subacute postinfectious cough. Low-risk component techniques can still inform self-management while avoiding claims of proven cure.
6. Australian TGA sources confirm pholcodine products were cancelled/removed after anaphylaxis safety concerns and codeine medicines are prescription-only.
7. Cochrane evidence does not show consistent benefit from heated humidified air/steam for common-cold symptoms.

## Search limitations

- No high-quality dataset was found giving reliable cumulative resolution percentages specifically for postinfectious cough at weeks 3, 4, 6 and 8. Acute-cough cohorts should not be used to manufacture such a curve.
- Medicine scheduling can differ by ingredient, strength and pack. Product-level Australian status should be checked in Healthdirect/ARTG at publication review.
- Some chronic-cough recommendations are not directly applicable to subacute postinfectious cough.
- This log does not record database result counts and therefore should not be presented as PRISMA-complete.
