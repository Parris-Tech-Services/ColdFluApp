## Source use notes

- `cicada-2024`, Australian Asthma Handbook, TGA and Healthdirect should take precedence for Australian clinical/regulatory framing.
- `cmaj-postinfectious-2024` is a concise secondary synthesis rather than a full guideline, but its conclusions align with the weak/negative pharmacologic evidence base for undifferentiated postinfectious cough.
- The speech-pathology trials and behavioural meta-analysis apply to chronic refractory/unexplained cough. They support the mechanism and safety of cough-control techniques but should not be presented as direct proof that these techniques shorten a 3–8 week postinfectious cough.
- The EPA source is used only for humidifier mechanics/maintenance; local humidity targets come from National Asthma Council Australia.
- Product pages establish availability/label indication, not comparative effectiveness.
