# Postinfectious dry cough — claim/source map

> **Status:** AI-assisted draft; requires independent evidence and clinical review.

| Claim | Source IDs | Applicability / caveat |
|---|---|---|
| Postinfectious cough is generally defined as cough lasting 3–8 weeks after respiratory infection | `cmaj-postinfectious-2024`, `chest-postinfectious-2006` | Adult subacute cough; diagnosis is by exclusion |
| It affects roughly 11–25% of adults after respiratory infection | `cmaj-postinfectious-2024`, `chest-postinfectious-2006` | Estimate varies by population and method |
| Residual inflammation and increased bronchial sensitivity are plausible mechanisms | `cmaj-postinfectious-2024`, `chest-postinfectious-2006` | Mechanistic framing, not an individual diagnosis |
| Routine inhaled corticosteroids, bronchodilators and oral drugs have no convincing proven benefit for undifferentiated postinfectious cough | `cmaj-postinfectious-2024` | Does not apply when a specific diagnosis such as asthma is established |
| Adult cough >8 weeks is chronic and should prompt structured evaluation | `cicada-2024` | Australian guideline context |
| Chest X-ray and spirometry are recommended initial investigations for adult chronic cough | `cicada-2024` | Earlier testing may be warranted by red flags or clinical judgement |
| Spirometry ± bronchodilator response, FeNO and selected bronchial provocation help assess suspected asthma | `asthma-handbook-diagnosis` | Normal spirometry does not fully exclude asthma |
| Cough-variant asthma can present with cough without wheeze/dyspnoea | `cicada-2024` | More relevant to persistent/chronic cough than ordinary postviral cough |
| Acid suppression should not be routinely used for isolated cough without reflux features/evidence | `cicada-2024`, `chest-reflux-cough-2016` | If heartburn/regurgitation are present, reflux treatment is symptom-directed |
| Avoiding food within 3 hours of bedtime is a recommended reflux lifestyle measure | `chest-reflux-cough-2016` | Low-risk, but benefit for cough itself is not proven in everyone |
| Indoor RH around 30–50% is recommended; routine humidifier use is usually unnecessary unless air is very dry or medically advised | `nac-humidity-2024` | Australian environmental guidance |
| Ultrasonic humidifiers can aerosolise minerals and microorganisms; cleaning and low-mineral water matter | `epa-humidifiers-2022` | US device-safety source; mechanics likely generalisable |
| Steam/heated humidified air has no consistent proven common-cold benefit | `cochrane-steam-2017` | Not specific to postinfectious cough |
| Honey may provide limited short-term symptom relief but is not proven to shorten postinfectious cough | `nice-acute-cough` | Adult postinfectious evidence is sparse; much honey evidence is acute/paediatric |
| Strepsils are indicated for temporary sore-throat/mouth discomfort, not postinfectious-cough resolution | `healthdirect-strepsils` | Product indication, not comparative effectiveness |
| Behavioural/speech pathology cough suppression improves refractory/unexplained chronic cough | `speech-pathology-rct-2006`, `psalti-rct-2017`, `bcst-meta-2024`, `cicada-2024` | Indirect for 3–8 week postinfectious cough |
| Saline irrigation can improve allergic-rhinitis symptoms | `cochrane-saline-rhinitis` | Direct cough benefit not established |
| Dextromethorphan is available in current Australian pharmacy products for temporary dry-cough relief | `healthdirect-dextromethorphan` | Availability/label claim does not establish large clinical benefit |
| Codeine medicines are prescription-only in Australia | `tga-codeine` | Since 1 February 2018 |
| Pholcodine-containing medicines were cancelled/recalled in Australia due NMBA-anaphylaxis concern | `tga-pholcodine-2026` | Do not recommend use; anaesthesia history remains relevant |
| Pertussis should be considered with paroxysms plus post-tussive vomiting and/or inspiratory whoop | `cmaj-postinfectious-2024`, `chest-postinfectious-2006` | Exposure/public-health context also matters |
| HEPA filtration can reduce particulates and may help selected allergic-rhinitis patients | `hepa-rhinitis-rct-2020` | Not proven as postinfectious cough treatment |
| Empirical antibiotics are not routine postinfectious-cough treatment | `chest-postinfectious-2006`, `cicada-2024` | Exceptions require a specific bacterial diagnosis, including early pertussis in appropriate cases |

## Claims deliberately not made

- No precise week-by-week postinfectious-cough resolution percentages are asserted because suitable data were not found.
- No claim is made that humidifier use caused any individual cough.
- No individual diagnosis is assigned from cough timing alone.
- No product dose is recommended.
- No medicine is labelled “safe for everyone”; individual contraindications/interactions require product-level and patient-level assessment.
