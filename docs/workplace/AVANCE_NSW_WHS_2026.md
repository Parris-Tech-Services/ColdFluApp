### Emergency procedure

1. Raise the alarm using the building alarm or a clear verbal warning.
2. Call **000** when required.
3. Follow the Emergency Coordinator and evacuate by the nearest safe exit to the **Car Compound**.
4. Account for workers and visitors and report anyone missing.
5. Do not re-enter until authorised.
6. For a medical emergency, Joshua Parris or another trained first aider provides first aid within their training, uses the kitchen first aid kit and calls 000 when required.
7. For fire, electrical or other immediate danger, do not take unsafe action. Isolate or evacuate and use emergency equipment only if trained and it is safe to do so.

### Why Category 2

SIRA defines a **Category 1** employer as an employer that meets at least one of these tests:

- basic tariff premium over $50,000 a year;
- self-insured; or
- insured by a specialised insurer and has more than 20 employees.

All employers that do not meet the Category 1 criteria are Category 2. Avance has only **3 known staff**, is insured through icare rather than being self-insured or a specialised-insurer employer with more than 20 workers, and its known small IT/MSP workforce is consistent with Category 2. Re-check the classification if Avance's staffing, insurer or premium position materially changes.

### Injury reporting and recovery process

1. Report every work-related injury or illness to Andrew Johnston or Joshua Parris as soon as practicable.
2. Record it in Avance's injury register, whether or not a workers compensation claim is made.
3. Avance must notify its insurer / Claims Service Provider of a workplace injury or illness within the required timeframe; current SIRA guidance states **within 48 hours**.
4. For a death, serious injury/illness or dangerous incident, notify SafeWork NSW immediately on **13 10 50** and preserve the site where legally required.
5. Support safe recovery at work and suitable duties where reasonably practicable, consistent with medical capacity and the insurer's injury-management requirements.
6. Keep medical and claim information confidential and limit access to people who genuinely need it.

## 2026 NSW legal baseline

- **Work Health and Safety Act 2011 (NSW)**.
- **Work Health and Safety Regulation 2025 (NSW)**, which replaced the 2017 Regulation.
- From **1 July 2026**, section 26A of the WHS Act makes approved Codes of Practice the minimum performance standard unless another method provides an equivalent or higher standard of WHS.
- Relevant SafeWork NSW Codes of Practice include first aid, consultation/cooperation/coordination, work environment and facilities, hazardous manual tasks and other codes relevant to Avance's actual work.
- NSW workers compensation / injury-management obligations include maintaining an injury register, providing injury/claim information to workers, having a return-to-work program, and nominating a person responsible for recovery at work for a Category 2 employer.
