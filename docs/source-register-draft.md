# Authoritative source register (draft)

Candidate sources for future source records, by domain. Every entry still needs the full source-record shape from `docs/CONTENT_SCHEMA.md` — exact title, URL, accessed date, scope and verification status.

**Links and programmes change. Verify currency at time of use.** Organisation homepages are discovery starting points, not substitutes for exact source records.

## Australian — clinical guidance

- Therapeutic Guidelines — subscription
- Australian Medicines Handbook — subscription
- Australian Prescriber
- Australian Immunisation Handbook
- RACGP guidelines and the Australian Journal of General Practice
- Australian Asthma Handbook, National Asthma Council Australia
- Lung Foundation Australia clinical resources, including CICADA
- Thoracic Society of Australia and New Zealand position statements
- HealthPathways — region-specific, clinician-facing

## Australian — regulator and government

- Therapeutic Goods Administration — product registration, recalls and safety alerts
- Australian Commission on Safety and Quality in Health Care
- Australian Centre for Disease Control
- Department of Health, Disability and Ageing
- State and territory health departments — exclusion periods, escalation routes and local public-health guidance

## Australian — consumer-facing, clinically reviewed

- healthdirect
- Royal Children's Hospital Melbourne — Kids Health Info
- Sydney Children's Hospitals Network fact sheets
- Raising Children Network
- Pregnancy, Birth and Baby

## First aid and resuscitation

- Australian Resuscitation Council / ANZCOR guidelines
- St John Ambulance Australia
- SafeWork NSW for workplace first-aid obligations and codes

**Special rule:** first-aid and resuscitation material is safety-critical. Any future module must be built from current authoritative Australian guidance, preserve exact source provenance, and receive appropriate accountable review before publication.

## International evidence

- Cochrane Library
- CHEST / American College of Chest Physicians
- European Respiratory Society
- NICE
- WHO
- PubMed / MEDLINE

## Not acceptable as evidence records by themselves

- AI-generated text, including this project's own agent returns
- Search-engine snippets
- Consumer health sites with no traceable source basis
- Vendor and supplement marketing content
- Organisation homepages cited in place of an exact document
