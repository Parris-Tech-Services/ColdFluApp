# Health topic master roadmap

**Status:** planning inventory, not reviewed health advice  
**Last expanded:** 15 September 2026  
**Purpose:** turn the original cold-and-flu research base into a broad, evidence-led Australian-first health reference while preserving the existing publication and clinical-review gates.

> This is a roadmap of topics to research, not a claim that the repository already contains trustworthy guidance on all of them. A topic is not public health advice until it has passed the repository's evidence, clinical, editorial and accessibility review process.

## Legend

- ✅ **Existing structured work** — canonical module or dedicated research package exists.
- 🟡 **Existing scope / partial work** — already named in the original programme, imported research or related source records, but not a reviewed module.
- 🆕 **Recovered expansion topic** — repeatedly arose in previous project work or health discussions and belongs in the broader programme.
- ⬜ **Coverage gap** — important practical health topic not yet represented in the repository.
- **P0** — safety-critical / high-consequence.
- **P1** — common, high-value everyday health reference.
- **P2** — useful specialist, preventive or deeper-learning topic.

The aim is comprehensive practical coverage for households, first aiders, carers and health workers. It is **not possible to enumerate literally every disease in medicine**; rare-disease and specialist content should be added through a governed request-and-review process rather than pretending this list is exhaustive.

## Original 28-module respiratory programme

### Symptoms

- 🟡 Cough — dry and productive.
- ✅ Sore throat.
- 🟡 Runny or blocked nose.
- 🟡 Fever.
- 🟡 Fatigue and body aches.
- 🟡 Sinus pressure and headache.
- 🟡 Loss of taste or smell.
- 🟡 Ear pressure or blockage.

### Self-care and treatments

- 🟡 Hydration.
- 🟡 Humidity and steam.
- 🟡 Rest, sleep and positioning.
- 🟡 Nutrition during illness.
- 🟡 OTC medicines by active ingredient.
- 🟡 Complementary and traditional remedies.
- 🟡 Gargles, saline sprays and nasal irrigation.
- 🟡 Lozenges and throat sprays.
- 🟡 Warm compresses.
- 🟡 Prescription antivirals and timing.

### Populations and conditions

- ✅ Babies and young children.
- ✅ Pregnancy and breastfeeding.
- ✅ Older and immunocompromised people.
- ✅ Asthma, COPD and chronic respiratory conditions.
- ✅ Cardiovascular disease and high blood pressure.

### Prevention and household health

- 🟡 Hand hygiene, surfaces, ventilation and household precautions.
- 🟡 Sleep, activity, stress and general health.
- 🟡 Influenza vaccination.

### Decisions and triage

- ✅ Self-care vs pharmacist vs primary care vs urgent vs emergency care.
- 🟡 Cold vs influenza vs COVID-19 vs strep throat vs allergy comparison, without symptom-only diagnosis.

# 2. De-identified topic history recovered from previous project work

The following themes have repeatedly appeared in prior health research, app work and conversations. This section deliberately records **topics only**. It must never be expanded into names, diagnoses, exact test results, episode dates, medication lists or other personal/family health history in this public repository.

- 🆕 First aid and workplace response: DRSABCD, CPR, AED, choking, wounds, bleeding, burns, heat/cold illness, asthma, anaphylaxis, diabetic emergencies, suspected heart attack, bites/stings, snake bite, incident reporting and workplace first-aid systems.
- 🆕 Cardiovascular health: palpitations, tachycardia, arrhythmias, heart-rate interpretation, blood pressure, cardiac testing, exercise safety, red flags and GP/cardiology preparation.
- 🆕 HRV and recovery: RMSSD/HRV interpretation, resting heart rate, recovery trends, confounders, exercise load and the limits of wearable metrics.
- 🆕 Digital health: Health Connect, Fitbit/Withings/Strava-type data, sleep, respiratory rate, oxygen saturation, weight/body composition, pathology PDFs, data quality, privacy and AI interpretation boundaries.
- 🆕 Sleep: sleep hygiene, illness and sleep, insomnia, circadian rhythm, shift work, snoring/sleep apnoea, CPAP, child sleep, wearables and persistent fatigue.
- 🆕 Exercise and recovery: running/cardio, strength, heart-rate zones, gradual return after illness, overtraining, injury prevention and recovery.
- 🆕 Nutrition: Australian dietary patterns, dairy-free nutrition, calcium/vitamin D, fibre, legumes, nuts/seeds, healthy fats, protein, micronutrients, hydration/electrolytes, supplements and food safety.
- 🆕 Gut health: microbiome, prebiotic fibre, probiotics/fermented foods, reflux, bowel symptoms and evidence-vs-hype.
- 🆕 Child and family health: fever, medication safety, respiratory illness, ear symptoms, rashes, vomiting/abdominal symptoms, hydration, safe sleep, immunisation, developmental health and when a child needs urgent review.
- 🆕 Pregnancy/postpartum/breastfeeding: medicine compatibility, nutrition, vaccinations, common problems, maternal mental health and infant feeding.
- 🆕 Mental health: anxiety, panic, depression/low mood, stress physiology, counselling/psychology, crisis pathways, grief and relationship/family stress.
- 🆕 Neurodivergence: ADHD, autism/AuDHD, assessment, emotional regulation, rejection sensitivity, burnout/overwhelm, sensory needs, sleep, parenting, workplace wellbeing and neurodiversity-affirming support.
- 🆕 Mind-body and spiritual wellbeing: slow breathing, somatic/body awareness, mindfulness, prayer/contemplative practice and evidence boundaries between wellbeing support and medical treatment.
- 🆕 Multiple sclerosis and chronic neurological illness: evidence-based treatment literacy, nutrition/lifestyle claims, vitamin D, exercise, fatigue, mental health, family/carer support and complementary-therapy evidence.
- 🆕 Environmental health: indoor damp/mould, humidity, air quality, smoke, fragrances, allergens, heat, cold and occupational exposures.
- 🆕 Medicines safety: paracetamol/ibuprofen/aspirin, decongestants, cough medicines, inhalers, supplements/herbals, interactions, pregnancy/breastfeeding/children/older-person cautions and accidental double dosing.
- 🆕 Aged care and nursing: infection prevention and control, clinical deterioration, medication safety, wound/skin care, falls, dementia/delirium, person-centred care, escalation and clinical governance.
- 🆕 Preventive health: vaccination, cardiovascular risk, healthy ageing, screening, physical activity, sleep, diet, smoking/alcohol/substance risk and evidence-based longevity claims.

# 6. Definition of done for a health topic

A checkbox in this roadmap only becomes complete when the topic has:

1. a defined audience, scope and exclusions;
2. current Australian authoritative sources where local guidance matters;
3. systematic-review/guideline evidence for effectiveness claims;
4. structured extraction and claim-source mapping;
5. clear red flags and escalation boundaries;
6. medicines/population cautions where applicable;
7. evidence, clinical, editorial and accessibility review;
8. reviewed links to learning resources;
9. source freshness/review dates and update triggers;
10. a short knowledge-check/quiz when appropriate;
11. no private/personal health history embedded in public content;
12. no unsupported diagnosis, risk score or dosing calculator.

---

# 7. Explicit privacy boundary

Historical conversations are useful for discovering **which topics matter**, but are not a public medical record. The repository may capture a de-identified topic such as `palpitations`, `child-fever`, `breastfeeding-medicine-safety` or `ADHD-and-sleep`; it must not capture who experienced it, exact dates, measurements, diagnoses, clinician names, medications, private family context, or uploaded personalised reports unless a separate explicit privacy-reviewed decision allows that.

Personal health-data analysis belongs in privacy-preserving tools such as **HealthLens**, not in this public knowledge repository.
