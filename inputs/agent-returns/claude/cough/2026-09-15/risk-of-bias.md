# Risk of bias - `cough` (partial return)

**No risk-of-bias assessment was performed.**

No study-level appraisal tool (RoB 2, ROBINS-I, AMSTAR 2, GRADE) was applied, because no full text
was obtained for any trial or systematic review in this bundle.

The `evidence.certainty` values in `module.json` and `extraction.json` are the returning agent's
**provisional judgement based on snippet-level reading**. They are explicitly **not** GRADE
assessments and must not be presented as such.

## Bias concerns already visible at snippet level

These are flagged so a reviewer knows where to look first, not as conclusions.

| Source | Concern |
|---|---|
| `raeessi-2013-honey-coffee-ppc-rct` | Single research group, single centre. Earlier reports by the same group appear to include overlapping participants, creating a double-counting risk. The "control" was guaifenesin, an active agent, not an inert placebo. |
| `cochrane-2014-otc-cough-cd001831` | The review itself reports that many included studies were poorly reported, making risk-of-bias assessment difficult, and that studies were heterogeneous. |
| `cfp-2023-bronchodilators-ics-postinfectious-cough` | Secondary synthesis of syntheses. The underlying reviews mixed acute, subacute and chronic cough populations and multiple drug classes. |
| `speich-2020-ospic-protocol-with-review` | Reports that most of the six identified post-infectious cough RCTs were at unclear or high risk of bias. |
| `cochrane-2018-honey-children-cd007094` | Some included studies did not blind participants; overall evidence quality low to moderate. |
| `vertigan-2006-thorax-speech-pathology-rct` | Single-blind, not double-blind. Behavioural interventions cannot blind the participant. |
