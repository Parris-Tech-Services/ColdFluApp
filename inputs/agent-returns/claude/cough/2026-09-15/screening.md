# Screening - `cough` (partial return)

## Method

Screening was **informal and single-pass**. Results returned by the web search tool were read as
snippets and retained if they appeared to be a guideline, systematic review, randomised trial or
regulator record relevant to cough in adults after infection.

There was **no**:

- pre-specified inclusion/exclusion form applied per record;
- record-by-record screening log with reasons at title/abstract stage;
- second screener or reconciliation;
- count of records screened, included or excluded.

This does not meet the reproducibility standard in `docs/AGENT_RESEARCH_HANDOFF.md`. It is recorded
honestly rather than presented as something it was not.

## Inclusion criteria applied informally

- Adults, or explicitly labelled where paediatric.
- Cough after acute respiratory infection, or chronic cough where no closer evidence existed.
- Guidelines, systematic reviews, randomised trials, and national regulator records.
- English language.

## Exclusion criteria applied informally

- Commercial content marketing a product.
- Consumer health-site summaries with no traceable primary source.
- Patent filings and clinical-trial protocol boilerplate surfaced incidentally by the search tool.
- Paediatric-only evidence, except where retained and explicitly labelled as indirect.

## Records retained

20 source records. See `source-records/`. Retention is **not** an assessment of quality; several
were retained specifically so a reviewer can check whether they support what has been attributed
to them.
