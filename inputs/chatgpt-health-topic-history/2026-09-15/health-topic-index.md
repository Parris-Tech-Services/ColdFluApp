# De-identified health topic history index — 15 September 2026

**Provenance:** AI-assisted retrospective topic discovery from prior project/conversation context.  
**Status:** unverified discovery input only.  
**Publication:** never use this file itself as evidence for a health claim.

## Privacy rule

This file intentionally records **only the health subjects and resource/product themes that have been discussed before**. It excludes names, exact symptoms experienced by identifiable people, dates of health events, test results, measurements, diagnoses, prescriptions, clinician names, uploaded personal reports and family/relationship context.

Its purpose is to stop useful *topics* being forgotten while keeping personal health history out of a public repository.
